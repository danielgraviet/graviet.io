## Thesis

The real bottleneck is not horizon length by itself. It is horizon length without cheap verification.

## Notes

- RLVR works in math and code because truth is mechanically checkable
- Long-horizon autonomy fails when evaluation is expensive
- Progress may bifurcate between cheap-verification and expensive-verification domains

## Questions

- What kinds of verification can be decomposed?
- Which domains are likely to keep scaling first?
- What evidence would falsify the thesis?
